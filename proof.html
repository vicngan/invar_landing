What Fernwell is trying to do

HOW WE RAN IT

The fine print, in plain sight

What each side got

Plain Claude (claude-opus-5-5) got the exports above and this instruction:


      
It was asked for up to 10 problems, most important first, each with its numbers and a specific recommendation.

      
INVAR read the same company's records, joined them across tools, and worded its findings with the same model. Its numbers come from the data, never from the model: a sentence with a number the data doesn't support is dropped.

    

    

      
How we scored it

      
Each finding was checked against Fernwell's answer key, which was written when the data was generated. Our automatic grader credited plain Claude with 1 of 5, because it filed the workflow editor finding under a different label. We read every answer and credit 2. Pricing counts as a miss: Claude saw prices running below list, but never found that the doc and the billing system disagree.
